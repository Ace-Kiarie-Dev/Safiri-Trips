## Full description

Safiri Trips is a trip planner made for Kenya. Pick your stops, and the app works out the drive, shows the weather at every stop, builds your packing list and tells you the story behind each place. Save the whole trip and it keeps working with no signal.

PLAN IN MINUTES
• Search any place in Kenya or press and hold the map to drop a pin
• Add stops, reorder them, and see distance and drive time for every leg

KNOW BEFORE YOU GO
• 7 day forecast, sunrise, sunset and air quality for every stop
• A packing list built from the forecast and the length of your trip
• Optional rain and departure alerts for your trip

KENYA STORIES
• 42 original stories on places like Fort Jesus, the Maasai Mara, Lake Turkana and Karura Forest
• Best time to visit and a practical tip for each one

WORKS OFFLINE
• Save a trip with its route, forecast, stories and a map of the route area
• Made for the stretches of road where the network drops

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Your trips live on your phone, and you can delete everything in one tap

Safiri is Swahili for "to travel". Safari njema!

## Data Safety form answers

- Does the app collect or share user data? **Yes, minimal.**
- Data types: **App activity > In-app search history** is the closest fit for search text sent to our proxy. Mark as: collected, not shared, processed ephemerally, not required to be linked to identity, used for App functionality.
- **Location > Approximate location** and **Precise location:** collected, not shared. Optional (only when the user taps "Use my location" for a start point; "Near me" keeps it on the phone). Processed ephemerally: sent to our proxy to work out routes and weather, not stored. Purpose: App functionality. Not linked to identity.
- Note for the form: place coordinates the user picks by search are also sent to the proxy, but they describe places, not the user, so they are covered by the location answer above only when they come from the device.
- Data encrypted in transit: **Yes** (HTTPS only).
- Users can request deletion: **Yes**, in app (Settings > Delete all my data). Nothing is stored server side.
- No data sold. No data used for advertising.
