# Safiri Trips Privacy Policy

Draft for hosting at https://nesturex.com/safiri-trips/privacy (the app links to this exact URL).
Last updated: 16 November 2026

Safiri Trips is made by Nesture-X, Nairobi, Kenya. Contact: hello@nesturex.com

## The short version

- No account. No ads. No analytics or tracking SDKs.
- Your trips, stops and packing lists are stored only on your phone.
- You can delete everything from Settings > Delete all my data.

## What stays on your phone

Trips, stops, packing lists, settings, cached forecasts and place information, offline maps and scheduled trip alerts. We never receive these.

## What is sent over the internet

To show search results, routes, weather and place information, the app sends requests to our own server (a proxy run by Nesture-X on Vercel), which passes them on to open data services. These requests contain:

- The text you type in search.
- The coordinates of places you look up or add to a trip.

They do not contain your name, email, device identifiers or advertising ID. Our server does not store these requests in a database, and it does not log exact coordinates or full search text. Like any web server, it sees your IP address while handling a request, and uses it briefly to prevent abuse (rate limiting).

Map tiles are loaded directly from OpenFreeMap, which will see your IP address and the map area you are viewing.

## Location

Location is optional. If you tap the near me button and allow it, the app reads your location once to centre the map. It is used on your phone only, while the app is open. The app never requests background location.

## Notifications

If you turn on trip alerts, the app schedules notifications on your phone from the forecast it already has. Nothing is sent to a push server.

## Third party services

OpenStreetMap (Nominatim, Overpass), OpenFreeMap, Open-Meteo, OpenRouteService and Wikipedia. Each handles requests under its own privacy policy.

## Children

Safiri Trips is a general audience travel app and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

## Changes

We will update this page and the date above if anything changes.
