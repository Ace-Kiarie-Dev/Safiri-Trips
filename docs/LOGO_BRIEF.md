# Safiri Trips: Logo Brief

## Concept
A map pin whose inner cut-out is a flat-topped acacia tree at sunset. Pin = trip planning. Acacia = unmistakably Kenya. One simple mark that still reads at 48px on a Play Store grid.

## Rules
- One shape, flat vector, max 3 colours, no gradients, no text inside the icon.
- Must read at 48x48 and in single colour (white-only version for notifications).
- Not a safari truck, lion or camera. Avoid a generic travel pin on its own.

## Colours
- Pin body: acacia green #2F4A3A
- Sun: sunset orange #E2792B
- Acacia silhouette: sand #F4E9D8 (cut out of the pin)
- Icon background: sand #F4E9D8

## Wordmark
"Safiri" in Fraunces Bold, "Trips" in Inter SemiBold, small. Always spelled SAFIRI with an i.

## ChatGPT prompt (image)
Flat vector app icon, 1024x1024, centred on a sand #F4E9D8 background. A map pin shape in deep acacia green #2F4A3A. Inside the pin, a negative space cut-out of a flat-topped African acacia tree silhouette in sand colour, with a small sunset orange #E2792B sun circle behind the tree. Minimal, geometric, no text, no gradients, no shadows, no outlines, no photo style. Must stay readable at 48 pixels. Generous padding, the mark fills about 60% of the canvas.

Ask for 3 variations: (1) pin on sand background, (2) sand pin on green background, (3) pin only on transparent background.

## Refine yourself
- Redraw the best result as clean vector (Figma or Illustrator image trace) and simplify the curves.
- Android adaptive icon: keep the mark inside the centre 66% safe zone. Export foreground (transparent) and background (solid sand) as 1024x1024 PNG.
- Also export: 512x512 Play Store icon, a white-only monochrome version, and a 1024x500 feature graphic.
- Save to assets/ replacing: icon.png, android-icon-foreground.png, android-icon-background.png, android-icon-monochrome.png, splash-icon.png.
