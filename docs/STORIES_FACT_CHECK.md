## Check log, 19 Nov 2026

Checked with a fresh web search (most likely to change or be wrong):
- Ol Pejeta: Ol Pejeta's own April 2026 page still says the last two northern white rhinos live there. Rumours of their deaths only appear on crypto news sites, so the line stays. Recheck before each release.
- Chyulu Hills: fixed. Leviathan is the longest lava tube in Africa but only about 11th in the world, so the story now says "the longest lava tube in Africa".
- Sheldrick Wildlife Trust: the 11am to noon visit is confirmed. Booking is by email through the Nairobi office, and KWS now charges the park entry fee on top. Tip updated.
- Malindi pillar: 1498 or 1499, and NMK calls it the oldest remaining European monument in tropical Africa. No change.
- Bomas of Kenya: the daily afternoon show is confirmed. No change.
- Hunter's Lodge (Kiboko) and Trout Tree (Nanyuki): both still open. No change.
- Mackinnon Road Mosque: the tomb of Sayyid Baghali, a railway-era foreman. No change.
- Kibwezi Forest: fixed. The forest is run with the Sheldrick Trust and Umani Springs lodge is for foster parents only, so the tip now says to check access first.

Checked against well established facts (no change): Fort Jesus 1590s, Nairobi NP 1946, Treetops 1952, Longonot about 2,776 m, Kenya Lake System includes Elmenteita, Hyrax Hill 1930s, Olorgesailie 1940s, Koobi Fora late 1960s, Thimlich Ohinga World Heritage 2018, Giraffe Centre 1979, Sheldrick Trust 1977, Karura late 1990s, Kakamega the only tropical rainforest, Shimba the only sable in Kenya, Mai Mahiu chapel by Italian POWs in the 1940s.
