Safiri Trips is a trip planner made for Kenya. Pick where you're going, and the app works out the drive, suggests places worth stopping at along the way, shows the weather at every stop and tells you the story behind each place. Save the whole trip and it keeps working with no signal.

PLAN IN MINUTES
• Search any place in Kenya or press and hold the map to drop a pin
• Start from where you are, or from anywhere you choose, one way or round trip
• See distance and drive time for every leg and the whole trip

STOPS ON THE WAY
• Get up to 12 places worth a stop along your route, in driving order
• Viewpoints, parks, heritage sites and classic roadside stops like Hunter's Lodge and the Trout Tree
• Add any of them to your trip in one tap

KNOW BEFORE YOU GO
• 7 day forecast, sunrise, sunset and air quality for every stop
• A packing list built from the forecast and the length of your trip
• Optional rain and departure alerts for your trip

DISCOVER KENYA
• 42 original stories on places like Fort Jesus, the Maasai Mara, Lake Turkana and Karura Forest
• Best time to visit and a practical tip for each one
• Browse by Coast, Safari, Mountains, Lakes, Heritage and City

SHARE YOUR TRIP
• Turn your trip into a clean picture card with the route and stops
• Save it to your phone or send it on WhatsApp and anywhere else

WORKS OFFLINE
• Save a trip with its route, forecast, stories and a map of the route area
• Made for the stretches of road where the network drops

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Your trips live on your phone, and you can delete everything in one tap
• Light and dark mode

Safiri is Swahili for "to travel". Safari njema!
